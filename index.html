Jaro-Winkler: compares the characters in both names and gives extra weight when the names share the same beginning. It is useful for spelling mistakes such as Sydney and Sydnee.
Token similarity: splits each name into separate words and checks how many words are shared, regardless of word order. For example, West Sydney and Sydney West share the same tokens.
The combined score is (Jaro-Winkler × 50%) + (Token similarity × 50%).
